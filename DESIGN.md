# Design Direction: Buku Bekas

## Identity
Editorial bookshop marketplace for used books in Surabaya. Craft-focused, intentional, no AI aesthetic.

## Personality
Curated. Thoughtful. Local. Bookish.

## Palette
- **Paper**: #F7F4ED (cream)
- **Ink**: #16130F (near-black)
- **Muted**: #6B6459 (grey-brown)
- **Rule**: #DED7C9 (beige-grey)
- **Accent**: #8A2B1E (oxblood)

No gradients. No neons. No glow. Borders and rules over shadows.

## Typography
- **Display**: Instrument Serif (titles, headings, price)
- **Body**: Inter Tight (copy, labels, UI)

## Mood
Quiet. Tactile. Like walking into a used bookshop in Surabaya.

## Key Phrase
"Prioritize Surabaya Area Via Gojek Instant" — integration, not decoration.

## Dial
- **ENERGY**: 1 (calm, editorial)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (consistent with intentional breaks)
- **MOTION**: 1 (hover states, no animation)

## What to Avoid
- Gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Generic stock photos
- Decoration without purpose
- AI-default layouts
- Template patterns
